This Privacy Policy explains how Unblocked Games USA ("we", "us") handles information when you visit unblockedgames-usa.github.io. We keep this short and honest: this site is intentionally minimal and does not require an account, sign-up, or any personal information from you.

Information we do not collect

  • We do not run our own database of visitors on this site.

Analytics

We use Google Analytics to understand aggregate site usage (which pages are visited, roughly from where, on what devices). Google Analytics sets cookies and uses identifiers such as your IP address (truncated where required by law). You can review Google's practices at policies.google.com/privacy, and you may opt out of Google Analytics via Google's Ads Settings.

Advertising (Google AdSense)

We display advertising through Google AdSense. Google and its partners use advertising cookies to serve ads based on your visits to this and other sites. Depending on your region, you may see a consent request before personalized advertising is enabled, and you can manage ad personalization at google.com/settings/ads. Third-party vendors, including Google, use cookies to serve ads based on prior visits to this website or other websites.

Third-party game content

The games listed on this site are hosted and served by third parties and open inside embedded frames on our pages. When you play a game, the game provider's own systems may receive your IP address and set its own cookies, subject to that provider's privacy policy, which we do not control.

Children's privacy

This site is a general-audience games catalog and is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13.

Your choices

  • You can block or delete cookies through your browser settings at any time.
  • You can opt out of personalized advertising with Google's Ads Settings.
  • You can browse the site in a private/incognito window to limit cookie persistence.
